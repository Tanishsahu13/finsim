The Industry Challenge

Why Payment Systems Fail in Production

⚡
Ambiguous Timeouts
An HTTP request to debit Bank A times out after 2.0s. Did Bank A commit the debit or drop the packet? A naive retry causes a duplicate charge.
🏧
Hardware Traps
An ATM initiates a $200 debit, but the physical dispenser jams midway after delivering only $80. Software ledgers must establish physical truth before compensating.
🔒
Closed Sandboxes
Real bank sandboxes simulate one provider in isolation. They forbid chaos injection, mock out real network partitions, and block independent protocol verification.

Mathematical Rigor

4 Uncompromising Design Invariants

1. UNKNOWN is a Definitive State
Outbound timeouts transition transfers to UNKNOWN, never FAILED. Replaying an UNKNOWN transfer returns 409 RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED until resolved via ADR 0001.
2. Double-Entry Conservation
Money is never created out of thin air. For every transaction across banks, ∑ Debits ≡ ∑ Credits. Floating-point math is strictly banned in favor of BigDecimal.
3. Deterministic Virtual Clock
Virtual business time is strictly separated from wall clocks. Simulate 30 days of interest calculation, hold expiry, and batch settlement deterministically in 3 seconds.
4. Strict Synthetic Isolation
Every participant, account, and card carries "provenance": "SYNTHETIC". Zero real PII or bank secrecy boundaries, making testing 100% reproducible for researchers.
